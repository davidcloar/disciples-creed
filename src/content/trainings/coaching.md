Coaching is provided to the pastoral leadership in churches and the senior leadership in para-church organizations. The coaching emphasizes the fundamental principles and foundational aspects of biblical disciple-making, along with an introduction to the Five Essentials of biblical discipleship.

## Session Topics

Topics are determined after an introductory and investigative conversation, selected from the topics below.

- Defining terms, goals, and the chief end of disciple-making
- Cosmic conflict: establishing the setting of the grand narrative, passive and active engagement, cruciform life and wartime stewardship
- Contrasting western (provincial) disciple-making vs. biblical disciple-making
- **The Five Essentials of Biblical Discipleship** *(one session for each topic)*:
  - Lovers of God & the Word
  - Apprehending the Glory of God
  - Union in Christ
  - The Kingdom of God
  - The Community of the King
- The biblical goals of disciple-making
- Community-based disciple-making and multiplying biblical disciple-making communities
- Discipling others towards Christ
- The practice of biblical disciple-making and discipleship
- Developing disciple-makers of the nations (two sessions)
