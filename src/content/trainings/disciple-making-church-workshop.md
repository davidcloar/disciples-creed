This 8-hour interactive workshop is designed for church leadership, encompassing both vocational and lay leadership. It can be conveniently adapted for use by para-church organizations or other mission-oriented groups.

## Workshop Sessions

- **Workshop One** — Introduction: Definitions, Direction, Movements and Goals
- **Workshop Two** — The Plot and Setting of the Story of God

**The Five Essentials of Biblical Discipleship** *(Workshops 3–7)*

- **Workshop Three** — Lovers of God & the Word
- **Workshop Four** — Apprehending the Glory of God
- **Workshop Five** — Union With Christ
- **Workshop Six** — Kingdom of God
- **Workshop Seven** — Multiplying Discipling Communities

---

- **Workshop Eight** — New Testament Principles of Application

## Recommended Schedule

- **Day One** — A 3½-hour evening gathering including three interactive sessions and breaks.
- **Day Two** — A six-hour engagement including five interactive sessions, lunch, and breaks.

This schedule can be adjusted to fit the church's schedule. The honorarium for the workshop is $1,200, plus all travel expenses covered by the church.
