Calling is a 12-lesson inductive Bible Study that investigates the three clarion summons of the Triune God to His people — a call to belong, a call to become, and a call to service.

Unfortunately, calling has become a mystical concept. When I hear most followers of Christ describe their quest for their calling and God's guidance, it sounds like an exhausting game of hide-and-seek with a transcendent court jester they call God.

However, I firmly believe that when we embrace the truth of calling found in the Scriptures, we will discover a place of unparalleled rest as we gain profound clarity about God's nature and His will and calling for us.
