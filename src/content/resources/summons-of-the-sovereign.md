The Summons of the Sovereign is a 5-week daily Bible study designed for every disciple of Jesus. It aims to help you establish the biblical footings for the maturation process of your new life in Jesus Christ. This is the first of five books in The Summons of the Sovereign series that focuses on Knowing, Loving and Delighting in God.
